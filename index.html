Manuel Ladron de Guevara

Senior Machine Learning Engineer, Higharc  ·  New York

I am a Senior Machine Learning Engineer at Higharc, where I have worked since February 2024 on machine learning for residential building design. My work focuses on how to represent buildings for machine learning models, and on models that furnish rooms inside a BIM (Building Information Modeling) tool: our DDEP model places furniture and fixtures in a room and runs in Higharc's product. Our paper on this work, BIM-Native Tokenization for Constraint-Aware Room Layout Synthesis, received the Best Industrial Paper Award at EUVIP 2026.

I received my PhD in Computational Design from Carnegie Mellon University in 2024, advised by Ramesh Krishnamurti and Daragh Byrne, with Jun-Yan Zhu on my committee. My dissertation, Parametric-Based Models for Artistic Representations, studied optimization and learning methods that paint with parametric brush strokes. During the PhD I was a research scientist intern at Adobe Research in 2021, with Aaron Hertzmann and Matt Fisher, and in 2022, with Daichi Ito, Jose Echevarria, Yannick Hold-Geoffroy, Yijun Li and Cameron Smith. I also taught as a studio instructor at the CMU School of Architecture.

Before the PhD, I completed a Master's in Advanced Architectural Design at CMU, focused on robotic fabrication with Jeremy Ficca, and a B.Arch and M.Arch at the Polytechnic University of Catalonia, with honors for my thesis project. I practiced as an architect in Spain, and I co-founded Flumio, a text-to-fabrication furniture startup, and CRAIDL, a creative AI group at CMU.
